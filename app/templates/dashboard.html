Tips & Tricks to Stay in Budget

  • Points renew every Sunday morning. Plan your week so you don't run out early or have too many left over.
  • Spend your points on any combination of items at any of the 14 dining venues (except Carson-based plan, which is dine-in only at Carson Dining).
  • Sample daily spending for 12 points:
    Breakfast at DUX Bistro—coffee and a bagel with cream cheese—2 points
    Lunch at Bullseye Taco—pollo asado burrito—4 points
    Dinner at Fresh! Marketcafé—shrimp scampi with a fountain drink—5 points
    Add a piece of fruit, milk, or cookies—1 point
  • Choose a plan that fits your eating habits. Deluxe (95), Standard (80), Select (65), and Mini (50) all include $50 Duck Bucks per term. Or Carson-based plan: Entry to Carson Dining any time, plus 5 points/week and $50 Duck Bucks/term. Most economical for frequent Carson diners.
  • Some dining halls may have limited hours during holidays. See Holidays and Breaks info.

  • Rollover: Up to 50 unused points roll over each week, but reset at the start of a new term. Rollover points can only be used after using your weekly allotment.

In case you have questions about your point balance, check it on My Housing or at any dining venue.
